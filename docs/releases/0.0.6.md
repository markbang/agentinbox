AgentInbox's floating layer is now Liquid Glass, the iOS 26 material: the bars, sheets and the compose button float over the mail and bend what scrolls behind them. Mail itself stays on solid paper. Search on a large mailbox is also 3–6× faster.

## What's in 0.0.6

### Added

- **Liquid Glass.** The floating layer is Apple's iOS 26 material from
  `nsh-cupertino` 0.3.5: the navigation bar and the tab bar float over the content,
  which scrolls under them, and they blur and refract what passes behind them on
  the GPU, with a specular rim that follows the light. Sheets, action sheets and
  alerts are glass too, as is the search field where it floats over results (the
  Search tab) and Mail's floating compose button above the tab bar. Nothing else
  is: list rows, message bodies and page backgrounds stay solid.
- **Compose is a sheet** on every shell, as Mail has it, with the app's glass behind it on a phone.
- **A floating compose button** of glass over the inbox, above the tab bar.
- **Settings has a large title** that scrolls away under its bar, as iOS's pages
  do (the library's `NavPage`).
- Selection lives in the glass navigation bar now (Cancel, Archive, Delete, Read,
  with the count as the title) instead of in a second strip under it.

### Improved

- Search on a large mailbox: 3–6× faster at 50,000 messages (1.7 ms for a word
  that matches 5,000 of them, 10 ms for one that matches every message, 0.6 ms for
  a quoted phrase, where before it was 9, 54 and 1.7 ms), with paging past the
  first 50 results (`before`) and quoted phrases. CJK substrings, prefixes and
  accented words are covered by tests.
- The tab bar floats over the lists, as Mail's does: rows pass under it, and its
  labels sit above the gesture area, with the bar's glass behind both. In our
  emulator screenshots the labels sit 13 dp above the screen edge, because that
  emulator reports no bottom safe area (NSH reads the top inset and zero at the
  bottom), so the gesture strip is not held back there; real phones report the
  inset, and the bar keeps its room.
